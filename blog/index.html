The H100 Rental Trap: Why $2.13/GPU-Hour Is Not the Price You Pay

The $2.134 H100 SXM headline is attached to a two-GPU bundle priced at $4.269/hour. An eight-hour fine-tune that fits on one card still buys 16 GPU-hours. This teardown walks through the smallest rentable unit, the 48.3% community-to-secure premium, utilization below 100%, and the one incident that can erase a 20.7% saving.

  • $2.134 headline → $4.269 per useful GPU-hour
  • 60% utilization → $3.56 per useful hour
  • 45-min outage: $48.75 of a $55.60 saving
