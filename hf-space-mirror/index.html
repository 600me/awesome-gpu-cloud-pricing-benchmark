Topology check

Fixed fact: RTX 4090 and L40S have no NVLink. Multi-GPU tensor parallel on those cards all-reduces over PCIe and can lose most of the scaling. A100/H100/H200 SXM use NVLink; one NVLink domain is typically 8 GPUs, beyond which InfiniBand and pipeline parallelism matter.

Method: weights = total parameters × bytes/parameter. GQA KV = 2 × layers × KV heads × head dim × 2 bytes × tokens × concurrent sequences. DeepSeek MLA KV = layers × (512 latent + 64 RoPE) × 2 bytes × tokens × sequences. Total = (weights + KV + 2 GiB CUDA) × 1.15. All memory units are GiB (2³⁰ bytes) unless labeled GB.

Limits: no activation peak, fragmentation, tensor-parallel sharding overhead, expert routing buffers, CUDA graphs or allocator slack beyond the 15% margin. Long-context prefill and training need more. This is a planning tool, not a deployment guarantee.
